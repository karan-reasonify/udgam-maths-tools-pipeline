# Preflight: what tools are failed on (build it right the first time)

Read this FIRST and whole; it is one page. Each rule names the check row that decides it (`docs/CHECKS.md` has the row). A number here is
a pointer: **the check decides**, so if a number and a check ever differ, the check is right. Rules marked *(silent)* are for a
SILENT runtime (the bubble is the guide, nothing is spoken; `readlist.md` says which your chapter is); on a VOICED runtime read
`PATTERNS.md` §9v for the voice rules instead. A rule about a feature (guide steps, Next key, card answer) applies only when the pinned runtime has it.

## The lesson *(silent)*
1. **One sentence, one bubble page.** Every instruction and every hint (a step's `say` and `hint`, a question's `say`, every `miss` and ladder rung) is ONE sentence that fits ONE page (`one-page`: English FAILS, Hindi and Gujarati WARN). A hint is at most 80 characters (the check decides), about 40 when its instruction takes two lines (`hint-fit`). Each piece at most 20 words and 110 characters, no en or em dash (`voice-lines`). No line twice in a phase, a near copy counts (`bubble-no-repeat`).
2. **Hindi and Gujarati: ONE instruction line + ONE hint line.** *(Classic runtime: the bubble holds the pinned instruction and the hint under it. A runtime with the new look (`keyOnly`, LAYOUT §1d "One sentence a screen") holds ONE sentence: the hint REPLACES the question until the child's next tap, so judge each of the two on its own, as a plain line.)* In hi/gu each should take one bubble line (`hint-fit` warns by name, `hint-lines-clear` reads the ink: three lines touch the outline). Shorten the TR line, never the English one the checks read.
3. **The ladder says more each time.** `miss`, `ladder[0]`, `ladder[1]` all different, none the answer (`hint-ladder`, `hint-ladder-monotonic`). The hint shows under the pinned instruction (with the new look, in its place) and stays until the child acts; `Work` never says something over it (`hint-pinned`).
4. **Win and closing lines.** A win line (`api.say`) is ONE sentence, or the question gets a `winMs` that covers its reading time (`classroom-pieces` decides: about 2.4 s a short sentence, 68 ms a character). Closing lines stay short. `learned` starts "You learned" and is at most 72 characters (`learned-lines`).
5. **Guide steps** (when the teach lines are acts): `steps: [{ say, on, hint }]`, ONE click on ONE control a step, a hint each (unique in the phase, not its own `say`). `api.did(name)` at EVERY click on a control or piece; register `api.onEnter` so Enter, `toolApi.check()` and `solve()` perform the step; make an act safe to start twice (`guide-steps`).
6. **Controls are the runtime's** (`Hit`, `Key`, `RoundControl`, or `control(id, label)` spread on a piece), each with a `data-control` id, labelled, at least 72 x 72 (`finger-boxes`, `no-crowding`). Everything else in the working area is plain: no `onClick` without a `data-control`, never a tool rect with an `onClick` over the floor (`guide-steps-real-wrong` clicks inert points, the empty board and digits for real at every step). Name a step's button after its act.
7. **Opt-ins.** Keep the skeleton's `board: true` and `nextKey: true` (`board-panel`, `board-fit`, `next-key`): nothing moves on by itself, so `Work` never advances or resets the picture on a timer. A tool whose questions take fraction or decimal answers also sets `cardAnswer: true` (once, on the tool) and draws the card as PATTERNS §9e says (`card-answer`).
8. **No free play between phases.** Free play opens only from the summary page (`no-free-between-phases`); in free play `item` is the last question, so read `stage === "free"` before any per-question flag.

## The picture and the maths
9. **Before the first answer nothing prints the answer**: not a value, a total, a reference, a step, a hint, a ladder rung, a teach line (`answer-not-echoed`; the maths reviewer reads the rest). **After a miss** the traceback redraws the maths and ends on the whole right answer, inside `pointerEvents="none"`, unmarked: no red, no cross, no word "wrong" (`wrong-is-unmarked`, `miss-shows-answer`).
10. **Exactly three questions a phase** (`score-honesty`); use the plan's `qid` where a plan question fits.
11. **Text and zones. A small picture is scaled UP, not left small.** A picture of one or two small things fills at least about a third of the working area (digits 64 or more, a card 110 or more, a meaningful label 40 or more; `picture-scale` warns): the last bench must read it. No text below the floor, 28 stage units (`type-floor`); contrast (`text-contrast`); working-area labels at most 4 words (`labels-short`); nothing leaves its zone, overlaps or leaves the stage (`zones`, `no-overlap`, `on-stage`). **The same screens are judged on a phone (844 x 390) in English, Hindi AND Gujarati** (`phone-frame`, `phone-type`, and the rows above on those frames): the stage only scales there (28 units = 12.1 px), so a Hindi or Gujarati label longer than its English one that runs into its neighbour FAILS, as does any text under 11.5 px; give a long label room or a shorter `TR` word, never a smaller size.
12. **Pictures: your own shapes, plus `<Skin>`** (`no-raster-in-tool`, `no-raster`, no emoji). Every `<Skin name>` exists in `images.tsx` and the plain drawing under it is the whole drawing (`skin-keys`, `skins-fallback`). Never read or edit `images.tsx`. **Real objects, not plain blocks** (`docs/IMAGES.md`, every tool, no prompt needed): draw each thing the child shares, cuts, counts, pours, weighs or measures as its own element with a clear box, and where `images.tsx` holds a skin for it draw `<Skin>` over EVERY such piece (the plain shape is only the fallback, never the design); a block left plain where the image pass made an object for it is a fault the visual reviewer reports. **People are drawn with `<Skin name=...>` from the figure skins, never as SVG; keep the figure at its own aspect (the `made.w` x `made.h` of its images.json entry; `Skin` slices any other box) and clip nothing.**
13. **Host scope** (`host-scope`, `host-names`): no `export default function`, no storage or network, no bare `Image`, `File`, `Lock` or `Infinity`; a shared field is never named `key`; no helper named like the runtime's (`words`, `indian`...); drawing helpers inside `Work` are called, `{Sheet()}`, never mounted, `<Sheet/>`.
14. **Indian commas from 1,000** everywhere, bubble lines included (`indian()`; the reviewers read it).

## The three languages
15. **`TR` for EVERY printed string** (`tr-coverage`): `TOOL_NAME`, each `concept`, steps' `say` and `hint`, `ladder`, `closing`, `learned`, each item's `say` / `ask` / `miss`, choice and `parts` labels, `FREE_LINE`, `START_HOOK`, each `api.say("...")` literal, and every word `Work`, `Thumb` and `format` draw (through `tx`). A line built at run time shows English in hi/gu unless TR has that exact line: write a literal per case. Keep hi/gu labels about as short as the English (`shots --lang hi|gu` shows the fit). Write TR when the English is final; an English edit after that changes its TR key in the same edit.

## The inner loop (the order that costs least)
16. While building run `node cli.mjs check <slot> --ch N --no-render` (seconds, no browser; the source and maths rows; it never certifies or ports). Fix every FAIL, then run ONE full `node cli.mjs check <slot> --ch N` (`C4_CHROME_POOL=2`; `CHECKS.md` says how long). A few rows exist only in a full run (`zones`, `hint-lines-clear`, `guide-steps-real-wrong`, `next-key`, `card-answer`, `phase-party`, `text-contrast`): fix what it names and run the full check again, but never chase a visual by repeated full checks. Use `shots` while building only for a layout question a row cannot answer (one sheet at a time), plus the one look at the ask and wrong sheets before you report.

## Not yours
17. Never run `clean`, `port` (a full `check` ports on a pass), `publish`, an upload or `voice` (silent: nothing to bake). Never edit the runtime, the checks, the docs, `voice.tsx`, `images.tsx` or another slot; never read another chapter. A `runtime-pin` FAIL, or a runtime gap, goes in your report. Never `sed -i` a tool file (it strips CRLF): use Edit.

## The new look *(keyOnly runtime: the readlist's features line names `keyOnly`; opt out only with a reason in your report; LAYOUT §1d-§1e, PATTERNS §16)*
18. **The question is only in the bubble** *(keyOnly runtime)*. There is no question bar: each item's `say` is the WHOLE question in ONE sentence, `ask` stays (checks and the sidecar read it) but is never drawn, and a hint shows instead of the question (`one-line-bubble`, `one-page`).
19. **Nothing under the key's corner** *(keyOnly runtime)*. Keep every element out of `box.key` (about x 1190, y 0, 282 x 88 in Work; the working area is 64,108, 1472 x 520): `const kx = box.key ? box.key.x - 12 : box.w` (`board-fit`, `key-only`).
20. **One sentence a screen** *(keyOnly runtime)*. Every instruction, hint, win line and closing is ONE sentence; digits print as written (`one-line-bubble`).
21. **A sequence of buttons is ONE key** *(keyOnly runtime)*, at the bottom centre, its label following the act (Cut, then Measure; never a key that only undoes), its `data-control` the current act, a 400 ms double-tap guard; every act goes through `api.act` (`action-anim`, `guide-steps`).
22. **An object looks like the object from the first frame** *(keyOnly runtime)*: the `<Skin>` is drawn as soon as the object is named, under the cuts and the shading (the visual reviewer decides; `skin-keys` only proves the keys exist).
23. **A round thing is a disc cut in wedges** *(keyOnly runtime; the owner, 2026-10-17; PATTERNS §17)*: a chapati, roti, pizza, cake, pie or clock is drawn ROUND and cut from the centre into equal wedges (the knife comes down on each cut line); only a ribbon, a bar or paper is a strip. Measure hangs the wedges in one row and they stay (no key that only puts them back); coloured wedges stay in place (the visual reviewer decides; `no-crowding`, `picture-scale`).
24. **A grid of unit boxes is dark, big and fixed** *(the owner, 2026-10-17; LAYOUT §3)*: every box has a dark outline (the empty ones too) and a counted box a deeper fill; the grid is the biggest square that fits beside the OPEN keypad (laid out from `BOARD_OPEN_W`, or `BOARD_OPEN_W_BIG` with `bigPad`, and `box.h`), clear of `box.key`, at the SAME size and place with the keypad open or shut (`picture-scale`, `board-fit`, `no-overlap`; the visual reviewer decides the rest).
25. **Labels round a grid are last-bench labels** *(same)*: counting numbers, caption chips, ladder names, card titles and word tiles are at least 32 (`T.label`; counting numbers beside the rows 40), weight 600-700 in the darkest ink, ONE line each, never shortened or dropped, a paper halo where they sit over a line or a colour; in hi and gu widen the box or move the label, never lower the size (`type-floor`, `phone-type`, `no-overlap`).
26. **A pencil that writes digits, signs or capital letters WRITES them** *(a runtime with the write move, `caps.pencilWrite`: every chapter fetched from 2026-10-22 on; the owner; PATTERNS §15)* (`pencil-acts`): every `api.act({ tool: "pencil", ... })` is either `moves: "write"` (`text`, `at`, `size`, `align`; no `ms`; the tool draws what it wrote with `<PencilText>` / `<PencilLine>` from the SAME geometry as the act, never a printed `<text>` popping in under a push) or says `marks: true` (it only marks, shades, colours or draws a line and writes nothing). The marks and the numbers of one step are ONE act (`{ dab }`). A translated sentence is never revealed by a pencil (the table writes `0-9 + - × ÷ = / , .` and `A-Z` only). One written act lasts 3 s at most. A chapter fetched earlier keeps its old pencil (the row is INFO).
27. **The answer is gated by the act** *(the owner, 2026-10-24; every tool; PATTERNS "Answer gated by the act")*: in a question with a real act on the working area (share, cut, check, take, lift, put together, tap cards into a bin, deal, count) the corner Answer key and the keypad/choice keys are disabled (flat, dimmed, untappable, no blink) until the child has done the act, then enabled, berry and blinking; a restart or the next question resets it, repeating the act does not re-lock. A question with no real act is left ungated and named in your report; never invent an act. Hints never name a key in the wrong state; a new nudge line ("Now tap the groups the top number names.") has hi and gu `TR`; the sidecar `.json` carries a `guide.answerGate` note (a remake regenerates the sidecar, so write it again). **No check reads this** (`answer-gate` only warns when `api.lock` is never called, and `check` and `approve` are `toolApi`-driven, which is never gated): the play tester and the visual reviewer judge it with real clicks, and a builder is failed on it there.
28. **A miss empties the slot; you write nothing for it** *(the owner, 2026-10-28; a runtime with `MISS CLEARS THE SLOT`, `caps.missClears`; LAYOUT §3, PATTERNS §7)* (`miss-clears-slot`): at the miss the runtime clears every typed digit, every part box (caret back in the first) and a picked choice, so `typed` is `""` from the miss on: run the traceback from `wrong` (`wrong.value` is what the child sent), never write the slot during the correction (`api.setTyped`), and a pad lent with `api.capture` handles `"clear"` by emptying its cell. On an older runtime the row is INFO.
29. **The question stays until the answer is typed** *(the owner, 2026-10-30; every chapter fetched from that day has `rules.questionStays: "strict"`; class 3 ch 9 C16 and C27 by hand)* (`question-stays`): once the acts are done and the answer is next, the bubble keeps the QUESTION, one statement at a time, and the blinking Answer / Enter key is the prompt. Never replace it with a bare cue ("Now tap the Answer key.", "Type your answer, then press Enter.", "Choose your answer."), and never let a timer or nudge swap it for another line before the answer is typed. After the child taps Answer the question stays too. In code: `api.say(item.say)` at the cue points, not a where-to-tap string. A nudge while the key is locked ("Type your answer first.") is fine..
