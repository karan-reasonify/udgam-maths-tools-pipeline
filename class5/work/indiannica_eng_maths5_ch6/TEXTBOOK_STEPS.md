## 6.1 Writing a fraction as a per cent (p. 92) - two methods, and only these
1. "Change the given fraction into an equivalent fraction with denominator 100. The numerator will then be equal to the per cent."
2. "Multiply the fraction by 100 and add the % sign."
- Example 1(a): 7/25 = 7/25 x 4/4 = 28/100 x 100% = 28%.            (method 1: both terms times the SAME number)
- Example 1(b): 3/200 = (3 / 2)/(200 / 2) = 1.5/100 x 100% = 1.5%.   (both terms divided by the same number: decimals, NOT used in the tools)
- Example 1(c): 1 3/4 = 7/4 = (7/4 x 100/1)% = 7 x 25% = 175%.      (method 2; the 4 and the 100 are crossed out: 100 / 4 = 25, written in red)

## 6.2 Writing a decimal as a per cent (p. 93)
"To convert a decimal into a per cent, just multiply it by 100."  Example 2: (8.26 x 100)% = 826%.  Example 3: (6 x 100)% = 600%.
Concept highlight: "Decimal and whole numbers can be changed into a per cent by multiplying by 100 and adding the % sign."

## 6.3 Per cent into a fraction / a decimal (p. 94)
- "To convert a per cent into a fraction, remove the % sign and divide by 100." Example 4(a): 10% = 10/100 = 1/10 (the 10 on top and
  the 100 below each divided by 10, shown crossed out in red).  4(b): 2 2/25 % = 52/25 % = 52/25 / 100 = 52/25 x 1/100 = 13/625
  (the 52 and the 100 each divided by 4, crossed out).  Highlight: "we divide by 100 and SIMPLIFY."
- "To convert a per cent into a decimal number, divide the per cent by 100 and write it in decimal form." Example 5: 52% = 52/100 = 0.52;
