# Write the Date: the owner's description (2026-10-28). Read with brief.md and ../NOTES.md "Owner decisions".

Develop an interactive tool that helps students identify the day, month and year in a date and write the same date in words and numbers.

Display familiar items such as a birthday card, invitation card or school notice containing a date. Beneath the card, show three clearly labelled boxes: Day, Month and Year. Provide a month selector containing all twelve months and a toggle to switch between writing the month as a word and writing it as a number.

Divide the activity into three stages.

Stage 1: Identifying the Parts of a Date. Students read a date displayed on a card and place the day, month and year into their corresponding boxes. Use clear labels to help students understand what belongs in each box. The tool should demonstrate that all three parts are needed to write a complete date.

Stage 2: Writing a Date in Words. Students select the correct month from a list of twelve months. The month name moves into the month box. Students then complete the day and year and read the full date in day-month-year order.

Stage 3: Writing a Date in Numbers. Allow students to switch the month between its word form and number form. Students practise converting a date from a format such as 12 July 2018 into 12-07-2018, following the chapter's day-month-year format. Preserve leading zeros where needed, such as 04 for the fourth month.

After students submit an answer, highlight each correctly completed box. If a part is missing or placed in the wrong box, provide a simple hint showing which part belongs there. Allow students to correct their answers and try again.

Visual and Interaction Requirements: Use clear date cards, large boxes and an easy-to-use month selector. Display the month names and their corresponding numbers accurately. Keep the day, month and year boxes in the correct order and ensure the numeric date follows DD-MM-YYYY.

Include new dates for practice and a free-exploration mode where students can select a date and see it written in both formats. Keep the interface simple and connect the activity to familiar events such as birthdays, school activities and invitations.

## Decisions on top of this (see ../NOTES.md)

Delete the scaffold's 4th phase. Dates must be real (no month has more days than it has; February 29 only in a leap year).
