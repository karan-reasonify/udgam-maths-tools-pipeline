## Notes for the builder

- Thumb (about lines 901, 913, 923 of tool.tsx) shows a unit in hi and gu only when `tx(x) !== x`. The symbols cm, m and km stay the same in TR (pattern section 11, and tr-coverage needs a non-empty entry for the `cm` part unit), so in hi and gu the phase 1 chip is empty and " cm" drops off every length thumbnail. Test for a TR entry (or show the symbol always), not for a changed string; or map the units to सेमी / मी / किमी and સેમી / મી / કિમી everywhere, cards and bubble lines too.
- The key labels Mark, Tens, Halves and Quarters come from KEYLAB; check in the fit sheets that the longest (Gujarati `ચોથા ભાગ`, Hindi `दस भाग`) fits the key.
- The runtime prints digits as words in hi and gu, so a key label must not be a number word: Tens is `दस भाग` / `દસ ભાગ`, not `दस` / `દસ`.
- Code-point counts overstate the width of Devanagari and Gujarati (about 0.5 em a character against 0.54 for Latin), so the long rows below are probably fine.
